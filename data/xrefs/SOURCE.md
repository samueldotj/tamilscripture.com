Format: tab-separated, header row `From Verse	To Verse	Votes`. Verse ids use
OpenBible book abbreviations with dots, e.g. `Gen.1.1`, `1John.1.1`; a range
appears as `Ps.121.1-Ps.121.2`. The pipeline maps these abbreviations to USFM
book codes via `data/books.toml`.
